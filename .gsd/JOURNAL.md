### 2026-10-24 — Project Initialized

Project conceived as a first-anniversary gift for Krishangi. Art direction established: mature luxury, cinematic restraint, editorial design sensibility. Inspired by luxury fragrance campaigns, fine-art photography, and architectural gallery spaces.

Key creative principles locked:
- Strict burgundy/ivory/charcoal palette
- Sculptural 3D forms over decorative effects
- "Every object must have a reason to exist"
- Stillness and negative space over visual noise
- Emotional weight through pacing, not effects
